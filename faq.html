Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping with us.

1. How can I place an order?

Visit the Products page, add your preferred items to the cart, and complete checkout.

2. Can I order more than once?

Yes! You can place multiple orders. Add the products you want to your cart and complete checkout for each order.

3. How can I check my order?

Open the My Orders page to view orders associated with your current shopping session or account.

4. How much is the delivery fee?

Delivery charges may vary by order or delivery location. Check the final total shown during checkout or contact us.

5. How can I contact the store?

Use the Contact page to reach us through WhatsApp or email.

6. What if I select the wrong product?

Review your cart before placing the order. If you have already ordered, contact us as soon as possible.

7. Can I cancel or change my order?

Please contact the store. Changes or cancellations depend on the order's current status.

8. How can I find out about delivery?

Check My Orders for available order details. If courier or tracking information is missing, contact us.
